What is it?

Sweet and sour chicken is a magnificent dish that combines chicken with pineapple and bell peppers to create a truly boisterous dish.
The combination of flavours is so unique that it keeps you wanting for more.

I find that the pineapple and bell pepper add a kick to it that you cannot get from Panda Express or any other fast food places.
The dish also pairs well with rice but it is great on its own too!

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cups water, divided
  • 1 (8 ounce) can pineapple chunks, drained (juice reserved)
  • ¾ cup white sugar
  • ½ cup distilled white vinegar
  • 2 drops orange food color
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 2 ¼ cups self-rising flour
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground white pepper
  • 1 ½ cups water
  • 8 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves - cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1 quart vegetable oil for frying
  • 2 green bell pepper, cut into 1-inch pieces
